Q: What types of devices do you repair?
A: We repair Apple and Samsung smartphones, tablets, watches and laptops. Other brands may be accepted depending on parts availability.

Q: How long does a repair take?
A: Most repairs are completed within 24 to 72 hours, depending on diagnosis and part availability.

Q: Do I need to book an appointment?
A: No. You can walk in, call, or book a doorstep pickup online and a rider collects the device from you.

Q: Do you offer doorstep pickup and delivery for repairs?
A: Yes, within Nairobi we offer door pickup and delivery. Outside Nairobi, you can send your device via courier.

Q: Is my data safe during repair?
A: We do not access or modify personal data, but we highly recommend backing up your device before submission. If you share a passcode it is encrypted, shown only to the technician on your job, and deleted when the job closes.

Q: Do you use original parts?
A: We offer both original and high-quality OEM parts. You may choose based on your budget and preference.

Q: Do repairs come with a warranty?
A: Yes. All repair work and replaced parts come with a limited service warranty, depending on the part.

Q: How do I place an order for parts or devices?
A: You can visit our shop, call, or place an order via WhatsApp or email. Payment must be confirmed before dispatch.

Q: Do you deliver outside Nairobi?
A: Yes. We ship nationwide via trusted courier services.

Q: What are your delivery options within Nairobi?
A: We offer door delivery or pickup from your nearest collection point (Mtaani pickup).

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Cash, M-Pesa, and Bank Transfer.

Q: Can I return a product if it's faulty?
A: Yes, returns are accepted within 7 days if the item is defective, damaged on delivery, or not as described.

Q: Do you sell refurbished phones?
A: Yes. We stock certified refurbished Apple and Samsung devices based on availability.

Q: Can I track my repair or delivery?
A: Yes. Every step of a repair booked online is shown live in your account, and we also provide updates via phone or WhatsApp.
